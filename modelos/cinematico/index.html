Prologue

Three rooms. One appointment at a time. Nobody rushing the scene.

Corvelle was built like a small set: low tungsten light, warm towels, no music unless you ask for it. Your esthetician stays with you from consultation to the last step.

Every running time and price is published below, so the only surprise is how calm an hour can feel.

Before your first visit

How early should I arrive?

Ten minutes is plenty. First visits include a 15-minute consultation inside the booked time.

Can I come wearing makeup?

Yes. Cleansing is the first step of every facial.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancel or move your appointment free of charge up to 24 hours before. In this example policy, later cancellations are charged at 50%.

Do you offer injectables?

No. Corvelle offers esthetic treatments only. If something needs medical care, we will suggest a board-certified dermatologist.
