A small studio, run by the person who treats you.

Marlowe Rue began with a simple frustration: facials that felt rushed and ended with a sales pitch.

So the studio keeps three treatment rooms, never double-books, and gives every first appointment fifteen extra minutes for conversation. You leave with a written note of what we did and what to use at home — usually fewer products, not more.

Before your first visit

How early should I arrive?

Ten minutes before your appointment is plenty. First visits include a 15-minute consultation inside the booked time.

Can I wear makeup to my facial?

Yes. Cleansing is the first step of every facial, so come as you are.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancel or move your appointment free of charge up to 24 hours before. Later cancellations are charged at 50% in this example policy.

Do you offer injectables or medical treatments?

No. Marlowe Rue offers esthetic treatments only. If a concern needs medical care, we'll suggest you see a board-certified dermatologist.
